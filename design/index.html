角力

Sumo, in One Character

Open callTypography, symbol & character · 2007

Sumo was once written 角力. Drop the 力 and the single character 角 is left: a wrestler, squatting. From there it charges and circles the ring.

To most people, just a mark.To anyone wearing lipstick, a kiss.

XXX, for Maquillage

AwardMainichi Advertising Design Awards 2006 · Self-initiated

In the West, XXX at the end of a message means kisses; in Japan few people knew it. Three letters became the whole headline of a lipstick ad.

Hair, Written — for TSUBAKI

Self-initiatedPoster series

The hair is not drawn, it is written: every line brushed by a calligrapher, one stroke at a time, the way you watch it appear here.

A Card Too Good to Throw Away

ClientWacoal maternity, Isetan Shinjuku

Advisors' greeting cards to expectant mothers kept being thrown away. The answer was a card that opens like a pop-up picture book: something to keep, and to show friends.

Leave No Trace — for a Toyota Hybrid

Self-initiatedAdvertising concept

Erasers become cars. As they drive out of the horizon across a pencil-drawn world, they rub out everything behind them: a car that leaves no trace, only clean paper.

Self-initiatedPoster series

A book gift card. What you give is not a book but what happens inside one: surprise, doubt, love. So the same open book keeps turning into a new shape.

good home — for Misawa Homes

AwardFinalist, Mainichi Advertising Design Awards · Self-initiated

Misawa Homes is known for design. When Japan was shaken by a scandal over falsified earthquake-safety data, these posters show the company honestly asking itself: what is a good home? A book, a puzzle cube and a block of tofu get scribbled roofs, puzzling it over with a smile.

Isetan, in Plain Sight

Competition entryIsetan · photographic poster series

In Japan, Isetan’s tartan shopping bag is recognised at a glance. A small square of that tartan sits in ordinary scenes, a hallway, a car window, a pavement, a bedroom, with one message: Isetan is already part of everyday life.

Characters, Words, Sentences, Pictures

PosterLecture by art director Kaoru Kasai

The talk’s title, 字、言葉、文、絵, read like the title of a book. So I designed a white book and photographed it, and made a logotype that sets the title’s readings beside the kanji in the Latin alphabet, like ruby.

An Ad for Toyo Bijutsu Gakko

ClientToyo Bijutsu Gakko · magazine advertisement

A school with a long history and tradition. The visual is a ruling pen borrowed from one of the teachers: a drafting tool worn by decades of drawing lines, standing for a school that has taught people to make things for generations.

Jiki — a Notebook for Time

RedesignEveryday goods · notebook

A memo pad with a clock face printed on every page. Draw the hands as you write, and each note keeps the time it was made. Its name, 時記, means a record of time.

Kissa Resto — Brand Design

ClientKissa Resto, café · logotype, posters & shop card

A single touch of green carries the identity of a small café: the green stroke inside 茶 in the logotype, a hedge and one big leaf on the posters, and a small leaf sprouting on the take-out poster.
